Apple requires `@rpath` to map cleanly inside `@executable_path/../Frameworks/`. If the `@rpath` search path is not explicitly set in the main binary's load commands, App Store validation will fail.

### 2. Embedded Dynamic Libraries Signatures & Framework Structure

Look at your `find` output:
