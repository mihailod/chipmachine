Published by the prominent state record label PGP RTB, it stands as the bestselling instrumental album of all time across the former Yugoslavia. The project is a pioneering work of Balkan electronic music, heavily featuring synthesizer arrangements, drum machines, and keyboard work. Its sonic aesthetic blends ambient, downtempo synth-pop, and smooth easy-listening electronic textures, drawing frequent stylistic comparisons to iconic late-80s soundscapes like Jan Hammer's Miami Vice soundtrack. Atari Falcon ACE Tracker 2 - Introduction	Atari Falcon	Thomas	https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZiW2wmFe2qo	https://img.youtube.com/vi/ZiW2wmFe2qo/sddefault.jpg	Released at Sommarhack 2014 for ACE Tracker 2.00. ACE Tracker is a highly acclaimed, 16-bit music tracker and software synthesizer built specifically for the Atari Falcon 030 computer. Developed by Thomas of New Beat Productions, it is considered one of the most advanced sound engines ever produced for the Atari community. Unlike traditional trackers that rely almost entirely on replaying pre-recorded audio samples, ACE Tracker stands out because it leverages a powerful internal synthesis engine combined with tracker mechanics. The software heavily relies on the Falcon’s onboard Motorola 56001 Digital Signal Processor (DSP) chip to calculate its rich synth engines in real-time. It uses the same core sound engine as its sister software, ACE MIDI. This allows musicians to fuse synthesized waveforms with traditional sample playback. It includes synth-level structures like dual oscillators, sub-oscillators, resonant filters, ADSR envelopes, LFOs, and built-in effects such as reverb and delay. Because it generates synthesized waveforms natively via the DSP instead of just stretching samples, it is widely praised for its incredibly pure and crisp sound output compared to vanilla trackers.

Metal Gear Solid - The Best is Yet to Come	Playstation	Rika Muranaka	https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6miaTf1gF4g	https://img.youtube.com/vi/6miaTf1gF4g/sddefault.jpg	"The Best Is Yet to Come" is the famous, hauntingly beautiful ending theme from the 1998 PlayStation classic, Metal Gear Solid. Written and produced by Rika Muranaka, the song is uniquely sung entirely in Irish (Gaelic) by traditional Irish singer Aoife Ní Fhearraigh. Game director Hideo Kojima explicitly requested a song in a language he had never heard before. Muranaka suggested Irish Gaelic because it was completely unrecognizable to him. The lyrics serve as a deep meditation on fate, free will, existence, and looking toward the future with hope after a life of conflict. Beyond the original game's end credits, a cut version appears as an unlockable iPod track in Metal Gear Solid 4: Guns of the Patriots, playing during Snake's emotional return to the snowy Shadow Moses heliport. The iconic opening bars were heavily sampled by the British metalcore band Bring Me The Horizon for their hit 2013 song "Shadow Moses".
