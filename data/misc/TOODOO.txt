GEMINI TAKE:

 Based on the notes in TOODOO.txt and my investigation of the current codebase, here is a report on the feasibility of
  implementing the top unsupported extensions:


  5. MGSDRV (.mgs) [610 files] & MoonBlaster (.mbm) [296 files]
   * Status: Unsupported.
   * Research: These are MSX music formats. MGS is Z80-based and MoonBlaster is sample-based. 
   * Feasibility: Hard. They require significant MSX hardware emulation (Z80, OPLL/OPL4, PSG). While source code for
     replayers exists (e.g., mgsplay), integrating them would mean bringing in a Z80 core and additional FM cores.

  6. Quartet ST (.4v) - Atari ST Tracker
   * Status: Unsupported.
   * Research: A 4-voice sample-based format for the Atari ST.
   * Feasibility: Moderate. There are C replayers available. Interestingly, the AyflyPlugin explicitly blocks files
     with /quartet in the path, likely because they often use the .ay extension but aren't Spectrum-compatible.

  8. Psycle (.psy) - Modular Tracker
   * Status: Unsupported.
   * Research: Psycle is an open-source modular tracker, but the .psy format is essentially a dump of the internal
     state. 
   * Feasibility: Hard. There is no standalone "playback-only" library; supporting it would require porting a large
     portion of the Psycle engine.
