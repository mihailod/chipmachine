# ChipMachine — Support

Support and help for the macOS app.

## Getting started

ChipMachine is a visual music player and collection browser for the music of
retro computers and games consoles. When it opens, it builds its searchable
index once — after that, everything is a keystroke away.

- **Just type** to search by song title or composer.
- <kbd>↑</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd> to move through results, <kbd>Enter</kbd> to play.
- <kbd>Tab</kbd> cycles filters — platform, format, collection. Press it again to
  drill into a group.
- <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>H</kbd> shows the full help screen with every shortcut.

## Playing your own files

Open local files from Finder, or drag them onto the ChipMachine window or its
icon. ChipMachine registers as an *alternate* handler for the formats it
supports, so it never takes files away from a player you already use — to make it
the default for a format, use Finder's **Get Info → Open with → Change All**.

## Do I need an internet connection?

For the online library, yes. No music ships inside the app — tracks are fetched
on demand from publicly available archives when you play them. Local file
playback works completely offline.

## Something not working?

Bug reports and questions are welcome. The most useful report says what you were
playing, what you expected, and what happened instead — and includes the version
from the title bar.

**[Report an issue or ask a question →](https://github.com/mihailod/chipmachine/issues)**

## Privacy

ChipMachine collects no personal data, has no accounts, and contains no analytics
or tracking of any kind.

**[Read the full privacy policy →](privacy.md)**
