# ChipMachine — Privacy Policy

*ChipMachine for macOS · Last updated 7 August 2026*

> **ChipMachine collects no personal data.** There are no accounts, no analytics,
> no advertising, no tracking, and nothing about you or your listening is sent to
> the developer.

## What the app stores

Your settings, the music index it builds, and downloaded files it has cached are
stored locally on your Mac, inside the app's own container. They stay on your
device, are never transmitted anywhere, and are removed when you delete the app.

## Network connections

No music is bundled with the app. When you play a track from the online library —
or listen to a radio station or podcast — ChipMachine requests that file directly
from the public archive that hosts it. It also fetches artwork and cover images
the same way.

These are ordinary web requests to third parties, so the servers involved can see
your IP address and which file was requested, in the same way any web browser
would reveal it. Those services are operated independently and have their own
privacy practices. ChipMachine sends no identifier of its own, and the developer
receives no record of these requests.

Nothing is requested until you press play. Playing local files requires no
network connection at all.

## Your files

ChipMachine runs in Apple's App Sandbox. It can read a local music file only when
you explicitly open it — through Finder, the Open dialog, or by dragging it onto
the app. It has no access to the rest of your disk, and the contents of your
files are never uploaded or transmitted.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children. It is a music player
with no social features, no messaging, and no user-generated content.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with an
updated date above.

## Contact

Questions about privacy, or anything else about the app, are welcome via
